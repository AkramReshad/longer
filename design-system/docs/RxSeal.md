# RxSeal

The double-ruled `Rx` stamp — a dispensing seal applied over product photography or in the corner of a panel.

Deliberately off-axis: a stamp pressed by hand, not a logo. The default 8° tilt is what keeps it from reading as a badge.

```jsx
<RxSeal caption="For oral hydration only" />
<RxSeal symbol="Rx" caption="Cohort 001" size="110px" rotate={-6} inverse />
```

Captions are dispensing instructions in miniature: `For oral hydration only`, `Take before activity`, `Not for parenteral use`.

Use sparingly — one seal per screen. It's an accent that certifies a surface, and it stops working the moment it repeats.
